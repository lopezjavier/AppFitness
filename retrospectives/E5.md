# E5 retrospective · Weight tracking

2026-10-21 → 2026-10-22 · `v0.5.0` · held 2026-10-22

Numbers and the lists below the user's section were drafted by the PM from the epic's history (PRs #128–#146, ADR-47…49, the E8 table).

## User's retrospective (2026-10-22)

The feedback is the same as in the E4 retrospective: everything works fine except the UX. The user keeps having the feeling that the app is being built as spaghetti: every new UI follows a different approach than the existing UIs. This is a known issue and it was agreed to fix it in E8. **Expectation: after E8 this does not happen again**, and `ux-reviewer` is refined so that the UI criteria are kept and UI/UX decisions stay consistent across the app.

## Numbers

- 13 tasks (T-076…T-081 planned; T-083…T-088 added from feedback) plus T-082 closure; 19 PRs, 4 migrations (`body_measurements` RPC, `body_circumferences`, Health Connect columns and `health_sync_state`, `profiles.target_weight_kg`), 3 ADRs (47, 48, 49).
- One checkpoint, agreed with the user at planning (it was five, then two, then one: E5-A was folded into E5-B). It needed three feedback rounds before approval: round 1 → T-084/T-085, round 2 → T-086/T-087, round 3 → T-088.

## What worked

- Cutting the checkpoints with the user before the breakdown PR was merged avoided the E3 situation: nothing was built past an open gate.
- Health Connect before the chart (T-079 before T-080) gave the chart and the weekly pace real data at the checkpoint, so the feedback was about real use.
- Fix tasks stayed small and sequential (T-086 crash, T-087 round 2, T-088 sparse weigh-ins), each with its own ADR when behaviour changed.

## What did not work

1. **The weekly pace and trend line were designed for daily weigh-ins.** The user weighs once or twice a week, so the 7-day average drew no line and the pace read "Sin datos suficientes". Found only at round 3, on the phone. The business rules had been written without asking how often the user actually weighs.
2. **Round-2 and round-3 feedback repeated UI inconsistencies** between cards of the same screen (`Objetivo` vs `Peso actual`) and between screens, as in E4. These are in the E8 table ("shared card component", UX consistency guardrails).
3. **A crash on deleting an old manual weight (T-086)** reached the checkpoint: no test covered a measurement outside the loaded month.

## Decisions

- ADR-49: the trend line connects consecutive points across gaps and the weekly pace is the change between the last two days with data, scaled to kg/week.
- No agent or `CLAUDE.md` change now. The `ux-reviewer` fix stays in E8, as decided in the E4 retrospective; the acceptance bar is the user's expectation above (no repeated inconsistencies after E8).

## Actions

- E6 planning: before writing rules over user data (rates, averages, streaks), the planner states the user's real usage frequency as an assumption and the PM confirms it, instead of assuming daily use.
- E6 planning: the epic-planner adds, for each task that deletes or edits records, a test with a record outside the screen's loaded range (the T-086 class of defect).
- E8: shared card component and UX consistency guardrails (already listed); the look-and-feel rows from the E5-B rounds are in the E8 table.

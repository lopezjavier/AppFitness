# ux-critic round 0: the design system (T-288, 2026-11-12)

Full report of the `ux-critic` review of the "Design system" gallery area (10 slices of one 360 dp catalog scroll plus 5 tablet images). The user **accepted all findings** on 2026-11-12. Dispositions live in `planning/ux-critic-ledger.md`, the accepted rules in `05-design-system.md` (in the ledger), the fix tasks in `tasks/todo.md` (T-289…T-295), the sources in `planning/ux-research.md`. This file keeps the evidence and the limits so later rounds do not lose them. Reference keys ([cmp-3], [col-2]…) resolve in `planning/ux-research.md`. Contrast figures are hand-computed from hex values (the planner re-checked them; see `05` §2.1a).

Summary: 19 proposals (C-01…C-19) and 4 rule violations (V-1…V-4). The ledger was empty, so nothing was dropped as a repeat. Biggest issues: colour roles (selection in two accents, a disabled primary that looks like an enabled secondary, low-emphasis text and input outlines under AA / 3:1); two component defects at 360 dp (set table clips "ANTERIOR" and gives REPS under 48 dp; the discomfort row breaks text mid-word).

## Rule violations (not proposals)
- **V-1, R-51.** Discomfort row in `estados_vac_o_y_error` ("Fila de acción y nota"): the trailing action is a bordered `GhostButton` (24 dp padding, 16 sp); production does the same (`feature\workout\DiscomfortSheet.kt:201-209`). The level text breaks mid-word ("Tensió/n o/fatiga"). R-51 asks for one line and "a text action". Fix inside the rule: a borderless text action (`bodyMedium` W600, 8 dp padding, 48 dp target).
- **V-2, §7 and principle 4 (targets ≥ 48 dp).** REPS field is 46 dp wide, 36 dp with RPE (see C-04); the `SetRowDefaults` comment already says "~43 dp at 360 dp".
- **V-3, §6 "Icons: Material Symbols (Outlined)".** `ic_info`, `ic_photo_camera`, `ic_photo_library`, `ic_label`, `ic_auto_awesome` are legacy filled Material Icons; `ic_nav_trainer` and `ic_health_connect` are unspecified legacy icons. Decision 2026-11-13: V-3 covers `ic_nav_trainer`, which becomes the Material Symbols Outlined `insights`. Filled is the app's state cue (active tab, favourite), so static filled glyphs blur it.
- **V-4, R-38 and §4.** The `sheetHandle` token exists although no sheet shows a handle; `glowLime` and `AppElevation.glowBlur` are drawn by no component although §4 lists the glow. Both appear only as `tema` swatches.

Already recorded and not re-proposed: T-280 gap 3 (English strings in `AppDatePickerDialog`) and the 45 catalog exemptions.

## Coverage limits (what was NOT judged; open for later rounds)
- Sheet, dialog, date-picker, action-sheet and message bodies are not rendered in this area (only their trigger buttons).
- Focus and pressed states and all motion cannot be judged from stills.
- Exercise images are placeholders (assets are gitignored).
- The 45 components in `GalleryManifest.designSystemExempt` are not shown (AppSwitch, AppIconButton, StatusChip, StepScaffold, StreamProgressState, ChecklistRow, CoachProposalCard, RpePickerSheet, ScaleOptionRow, OptionPickerSheet, SheetActionRow, AppAvatar and others).
- C-01, C-05, C-06 and C-08 change tokens those components use, so they need catalog entries before the emulator verdict.
- Rest-timer digits (26 sp) may be small at a distance, but no reference sizing them was opened; the logo is a brand decision, not a token change.
